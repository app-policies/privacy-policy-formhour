# FormHour Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 24, 2026**

FormHour respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the FormHour iOS app handles information.

## Information We Collect

FormHour does not collect, store on developer servers, transmit to the developer, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the IDFA.

## On-Device Data

FormHour stores the following information only on your device:

- Clock-face and display settings
- Tasks, projects, notes, due dates, and planned focus time
- Focus presets and Pomodoro settings
- Active Focus, Countdown, and Stopwatch state
- Intentions, reflections, focus ratings, and interruption counts
- Session history and locally calculated statistics
- Focus sound, volume, notification privacy, and daily reminder preferences

This information is used to restore timers after relaunch, show Today progress, organize tasks and projects, and calculate History and Insights. FormHour does not send it to the developer or a developer-operated server.

FormHour has no account or login and does not provide cloud synchronization.

## Backup, Restore, and Export

FormHour can create a JSON backup containing tasks, projects, presets, Pomodoro settings, and session history. It can also export session history as CSV. Export occurs only when you choose it and uses the standard iOS file picker or share destination. FormHour does not automatically upload backups or exports.

When you select a JSON backup for restore, FormHour reads and validates that file on your device, displays the item counts before import, and replaces local data only after your confirmation. The developer does not receive the selected file.

## Focus Sounds and Device Features

Rain, Brown Noise, and Soft Room are original procedural sound profiles generated and played on the device. Audio is not streamed, and listening activity is not transmitted. Focus Sounds stop when the session ends and use an audio-session mode designed to mix with other audio.

Quiet Chime, haptics, in-app brightness control, screen sleep prevention, and burn-in reduction also operate entirely on your device.

## Local Notifications

FormHour may ask for permission to schedule local notifications for Focus completion, Break completion, Countdown completion, and an optional Daily Reminder. Notifications are generated and scheduled on your device. Task names are hidden by default and appear only if you enable that option.

Notification permission is optional, and the app's main features remain usable if permission is denied. FormHour does not use remote push notifications and does not send notification data to the developer.

## Purchases and Advertising

FormHour has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertising.

## Contact Support

Contact Support opens an external email app with a draft addressed to **support@passmate.jp**. Sending the email is optional. The developer receives only the information you choose to include and send. Your email provider processes the message under its own privacy policy.

## Children's Privacy

FormHour is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

FormHour validates restore files before changing local data. Data remains under the protections provided by your device, operating system, and any storage destination you choose for exported files. No method of storage is completely secure, so keep your device software and access controls up to date.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if FormHour's features or privacy practices change. The revised policy will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact **support@passmate.jp**.
